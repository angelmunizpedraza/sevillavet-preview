Physical Therapy and Rehabilitation for Dogs

Physical therapy helps dogs recover from orthopedic surgery and injuries. It restores mobility, reduces pain and rebuilds muscle strength for a quicker, more complete recovery.

At a glance

Recovery that goes further

Physical therapy plays a crucial role in the recovery and overall well-being of dogs, especially after orthopedic surgery or an injury. Each program is tailored to the needs of the individual dog.

Our physical therapy team works closely with Dr. Pedraza to create a complete rehabilitation plan for every patient, so your dog regains function and enjoys a better quality of life.

For
Dogs recovering from orthopedic surgery or injury
Techniques

Techniques

How we help your dog recover

  • 01

    Laser therapy

    Low-level laser to reduce pain and inflammation and accelerate tissue repair.

  • 02

    Massage therapy

    Promotes circulation, reduces muscle tension and helps your dog relax.

  • 03

    Therapeutic exercises

    Customized exercises that improve strength, flexibility and balance.

  • 04

    Electrical stimulation

    Gentle electrical currents that stimulate muscle contraction and improve muscle tone.

Your dog's program

From assessment to full function

  1. 1

    Assess

    We examine your dog and review the surgery or injury, mobility and pain.
  2. 2

    Plan

    Dr. Pedraza and our team set goals and choose the right techniques.
  3. 3

    Treat

    Sessions combine laser, massage, exercise and electrical stimulation as needed.
  4. 4

    Progress

    We adjust the plan as your dog gets stronger and give you exercises for home.

After orthopedic surgery

Part of the recovery after TPLO and other surgery

After orthopedic procedures such as cruciate ligament surgery (TPLO) or fracture repair, physical therapy can help your dog use the leg sooner, rebuild muscle and return to normal activity safely.

Physical therapy FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

Which dogs benefit from physical therapy?

Dogs recovering from orthopedic surgery, such as TPLO or fracture repair, and dogs recovering from injuries benefit most. It helps restore mobility, reduce pain and rebuild strength.

What is laser therapy for dogs?

Laser therapy applies low-level light to the affected area to reduce pain and inflammation and to accelerate tissue repair.

Does my dog need physical therapy after TPLO?

Physical therapy may be recommended during recovery from TPLO. Dr. Pedraza will tell you whether your dog would benefit and when to start.

How do I start physical therapy for my dog?

Call (310) 310-3704. We will examine your dog and create a plan with Dr. Pedraza.
