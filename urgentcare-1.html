Online Pharmacy

Order your pet's prescriptions online through Mixlab, our exclusive pharmacy partner, and get them delivered to your door, often the same or next day.

Personalized for animals, simple for people

Your pet's medication, delivered

We partner exclusively with Mixlab as our online pharmacy. You create an account, request the prescription and select Sevilla Veterinary Hospital, and we approve it electronically through our online portal.

Deliveries take 1 to 2 days depending on when the prescription is ordered. Mixlab is local to Los Angeles, so delivery is very quick.

Questions

Online pharmacy FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

How do I order my pet's medication online?

Register or log in on the Mixlab website, request the prescription and make sure the hospital is Sevilla Veterinary Hospital. We approve the request electronically.

How long does delivery take?

Deliveries take 1 to 2 days depending on when the prescription is ordered, and are often same-day or next-day in Los Angeles.

Can I set up automatic refills?

Yes. Mixlab can refill your pet's medication automatically.

Can my pet's medication be flavored or compounded?

Yes. Mixlab offers flavored and compounded medications to make dosing less stressful.

Who do I contact if I have a problem with my order?

Contact the Mixlab team for help with orders and delivery. For questions about your pet's treatment, call us on (310) 310-3704.
