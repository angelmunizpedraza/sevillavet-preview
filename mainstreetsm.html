Santa Monica

Pet care in a neighborhood you'll enjoy

Our hospital on Main Street is just blocks from the beach, in one of Santa Monica's most welcoming neighborhoods. Boutiques, cafes and green parks are all nearby, perfect for a stroll with your dog before or after a visit.

Main Street is known for its eclectic charm, tree-lined streets and a mix of historic and modern architecture. Inside, the hospital is designed to make every visit as comfortable and stress-free as possible, whether you come in for a routine check-up or a complex procedure.

Sevilla, España

Named after our founder's hometown

Sevilla Veterinary Hospital is named in honor of Sevilla, Spain, the hometown of our founder, Dr. Antonio Pedraza. He was born and raised in Sevilla, a city known for its breathtaking architecture, vibrant traditions and deep sense of community.

When he opened his first veterinary hospital, he wanted to carry a piece of home with him. The name is a daily reminder of where it all began, and of the warmth and personal attention we bring to every patient and every family.

Questions

Visiting the hospital

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

Where is Sevilla Veterinary Hospital?

At 2116 Main Street, Santa Monica, CA 90405, a few blocks from the beach.

Is there parking?

There is no on-site parking, but metered street parking is usually available right in front of the hospital, and most spaces allow up to 3 hours.

Is the hospital accessible?

Yes, there is a ramp at the entrance. If you need help getting your pet inside, call ahead and our team will be happy to help.

Why is it called Sevilla?

It is named after Sevilla (Seville), Spain, the hometown of our founder, Dr. Antonio Pedraza.

Which areas do you serve?

Santa Monica, Venice, Ocean Park, Marina del Rey and the wider Los Angeles area. We also welcome referrals from veterinary hospitals across Los Angeles.
