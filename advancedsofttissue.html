Advanced Soft Tissue Surgery

Advanced surgical technology lets Dr. Pedraza perform complex soft tissue procedures in about half the usual time, with less bleeding, shorter anesthesia and less pain afterward.

At a glance

Complex surgery, gentle on your pet

Dr. Pedraza works with surgical devices that minimize bleeding, which reduces both anesthesia time and pain after surgery. With the right equipment and experience, he can perform nearly any type of soft tissue surgery.

Pain management is a priority: Nocita® is used for every procedure to relieve pain at the surgical site for up to 72 hours. Our highly skilled team focuses on pain management, anesthesia safety and current medical practice at every step.

In plain words

Common soft tissue surgeries explained

  • Pyometra

    A life-threatening infection of the uterus in unspayed females, treated by removing the infected uterus.

  • Cystotomy

    Opening the bladder, most often to remove bladder stones.

  • Gastrotomy and enterotomy

    Opening the stomach or intestine, often to remove a swallowed toy, string or other object.

  • Splenectomy

    Removal of the spleen, for example because of a mass or bleeding.

  • TECA

    Removal of the ear canal for severe, end-stage ear disease that no longer responds to treatment.

  • Cherry eye

    Repositioning the prolapsed gland of the third eyelid, which looks like a red swelling in the corner of the eye.

Safety at every step

How we keep complex surgery safe

  1. 1

    Assess

    A full examination and pre-anesthetic bloodwork; surgery is only recommended when it is the safest option.
  2. 2

    Anesthesia

    Careful monitoring, with ventilators and advanced protocols for higher-risk patients.
  3. 3

    Precision

    Devices that minimize bleeding shorten surgery and anesthesia time.
  4. 4

    Comfort

    Nocita® keeps the surgical area comfortable for up to 72 hours after surgery.

Questions

Soft tissue surgery FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

What is soft tissue surgery?

Any surgery that is not on bones or joints: procedures on the skin, ears, eyes, airway, abdominal organs such as the spleen, liver, bladder and intestines, and the chest.

Which soft tissue surgeries do you perform?

Head and neck procedures such as cherry eye, TECA and rhinoplasty; abdominal procedures such as splenectomy, cystotomy, gastrotomy, pyometra and hernia repair; and chest procedures such as lung lobectomy and pericardectomy.

How is pain managed after soft tissue surgery?

Every procedure includes Nocita®, a long-acting local anesthetic that relieves pain at the surgical site for up to 72 hours.

Will my pet stay in the hospital overnight?

Many pets go home the same day. Overnight monitoring is available on select days for patients that need extended post-operative care.

Do you accept soft tissue surgery referrals?

Yes. We offer free consultations for potential surgical referrals and keep the referring veterinarian informed at every step.
