Surgical center

Advanced surgical care in Santa Monica

Dr. Antonio Pedraza completed postgraduate surgical training in the United Kingdom, focused on orthopedics, spinal and complex soft tissue surgery, and brings more than 20 years of experience to every procedure. Our surgical suite is equipped for orthopedics, laparoscopy and endoscopy.

  • Imaging in-house, before and after surgery
  • Epidural and long-acting local anesthetic (Nocita®) for orthopedic surgery
  • Ventilator-supported anesthesia
  • Free consultations for potential surgical referrals

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on (310) 310-3704.

Where is Sevilla Veterinary Hospital?

At 2116 Main Street, Santa Monica, CA 90405, on Main Street just blocks from the beach. Metered street parking is available in front of the hospital; most spaces allow up to 3 hours.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 6:00 pm. The hospital is closed on Saturday and Sunday, but our phone line is still answered.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We offer $60 pet exams for both new and existing clients. Call (310) 310-3704 or request an appointment online.

Do you see urgent cases?

Yes, during business hours. Call us and tell us what is going on: we make every effort to see urgent cases as quickly as possible, and if a procedure is needed we can usually perform it here. Outside business hours, please contact a 24-hour emergency hospital.

Which animals do you treat?

Dogs and cats, from puppies and kittens to senior pets.

Do you perform TPLO surgery for torn cruciate ligaments?

Yes. TPLO is one of the most common orthopedic procedures we perform for cranial cruciate ligament rupture, and most dogs go home the same day or the day after surgery.

Who leads the hospital?

Dr. Antonio Pedraza, DVM, MVM, cert SAS, MRCVS. He was born and raised in Sevilla (Seville), Spain, completed postgraduate surgical training in the United Kingdom and holds a Master's degree in Small Animal Surgery & Traumatology from Madrid.

Do you accept referrals from other veterinarians?

Yes. We work closely with referring veterinarians, keep them updated with detailed reports, and offer free consultations for potential surgical referrals.
