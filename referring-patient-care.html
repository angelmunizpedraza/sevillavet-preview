Working together

A partner for your patients

At Sevilla Veterinary Hospital we are dedicated to strong, cooperative relationships with referring veterinarians. We value the trust you place in us and keep communication open and transparent throughout the referral.

Our goal is to complement your practice with advanced procedures that enhance the care your patients receive, and to send them back to you with clear reports and recommendations.

Consultations
Free consultations for potential surgical referrals
Reports
Detailed reports and progress updates at every step
Anesthesia
Ventilators and advanced protocols, including respiratory cases
Scheduling
Same-day surgery when medically appropriate
Costs
We coordinate with you to help minimize overall costs

Our commitment to collaboration

What you can count on

  • Advanced care

    Modern equipment and surgical experience for consultations, TPLO, complex diagnostics and minimally invasive surgery.

  • Communication

    Detailed reports and updates on your patient's progress for a seamless continuum of care.

  • A shared goal

    The well-being of the animals we treat, with care that addresses every aspect of their health.

  • Safe anesthesia

    Ventilators and advanced anesthesia protocols, particularly for patients with respiratory challenges.

Cases we welcome

How we can help your patients

Orthopedic

  • Cruciate ligament repair (TPLO, TTA, CBLO)
  • Fracture repair
  • Medial patellar luxation
  • FHNO
  • Spinal surgery

Soft tissue and airway

  • Brachycephalic airway (BOAS) surgery
  • Splenectomy and liver lobectomy
  • Cystotomy and urethrostomy
  • Gastrotomy and enterotomy
  • Hernia repair and mass removal

Minimally invasive

  • Laparoscopic spay
  • Laparoscopic gastropexy
  • Laparoscopic cryptorchid neuter
  • Laparoscopic biopsy
  • Endoscopy, rhinoscopy and cystoscopy

How to refer

Referring a patient in four steps

  1. 1

    Contact us

    Send the form below or call (310) 310-3704 with the case details.
  2. 2

    We reach the owner

    Our team contacts the owner to schedule a consultation, free for potential surgical referrals.
  3. 3

    Consult and treat

    Dr. Pedraza examines the patient, discusses options and performs the procedure.
  4. 4

    Report back

    You receive detailed reports and updates, and the patient returns to your care.

Questions

Referral FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

How do I refer a patient to Sevilla Veterinary Hospital?

Fill out the referral form on this page or call (310) 310-3704. A dedicated team member will contact you and the owner.

Do you offer free consultations for referrals?

Yes, consultations are free for potential surgical referrals.

Will I receive updates on my patient?

Yes. You receive detailed reports and updates on your patient's progress at every step of the referral.

Which procedures do you accept referrals for?

Orthopedic surgery including TPLO, soft tissue and airway surgery including BOAS, and minimally invasive and laparoscopic procedures, as well as complex diagnostics.
