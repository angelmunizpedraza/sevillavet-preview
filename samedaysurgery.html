Same-Day Surgery

At a glance

Your time matters, and so does timing

Medical problems don't always fit neatly into a schedule. Our morning fasted consultations allow patients to be evaluated early in the day and, when medically appropriate, to go ahead with surgery the same day.

We are very flexible with surgical cases and equipped for both scheduled and urgent needs, including emergencies. Every patient is carefully assessed, and surgery is only recommended when it is the safest option for that pet.

Benefits of same-day surgery

Why families and referring vets choose it

  • Convenience

    Evaluation and surgery the same day mean fewer visits and less time away from home.

  • Timely care

    Time-sensitive and emergency cases are treated without unnecessary delays.

  • Collaboration

    We coordinate with your primary or referring veterinarian and help minimize overall costs.

  • Experienced team

    A trained surgical and nursing team with standardized safety protocols.

  • Overnight care

    Overnight monitoring is available on select days for pets that need it.

  • Financing

    Flexible financing options make advanced and urgent surgery more accessible.

  • Insurance support

    We work with all pet insurance carriers and help with documentation and claims.

  • Less stress

    Fewer delays and appointments, and a smoother experience for pets and families.

Questions

Same-day surgery FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

What is a morning fasted consultation?

Your pet comes in early in the day without breakfast, so that if surgery is needed and medically appropriate, it can go ahead the same day without another visit.

Can my pet really have surgery the same day?

Often, yes. When the exam shows that surgery is the safest and most appropriate option, we can usually perform it the same day, for both scheduled and urgent cases.

What time should I drop off my pet?

Surgical drop-off is 8:00 – 9:30 am. We will confirm the time when you book.

Will my pet stay overnight?

Most pets go home the same day. Overnight monitoring is available on select days for pets that need extended post-operative care.

Do you offer financing and accept pet insurance?

Yes. We offer financing options, and we work with all pet insurance carriers and help with documentation and claim submission whenever possible.
