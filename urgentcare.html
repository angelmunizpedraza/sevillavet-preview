When to call

Signs your pet needs urgent care

Urgent care is for serious problems that need prompt attention. Call us if you notice any of these signs:

  • Pale or yellow gums
  • Difficulty breathing or labored breathing
  • Persistent or excessive coughing
  • Less tolerance for activity or exercise
  • Loss of appetite
  • Signs of dehydration
  • Unusual lethargy or weakness
  • Straining or difficulty urinating or defecating
  • Frequent trips to the litter box, or staying in it a long time
  • Sudden vomiting and/or diarrhea
  • Visible pain or anxiety
  • Swollen or closed eyelids
  • Sudden lameness or not bearing weight on a leg
  • Foxtails in the fur, skin, ears, nose or paws
  • Swallowing toys, string or other objects

A Southern California hazard

Foxtails: small seeds, big problems

Foxtails are grass seeds with barbed tips. They cling to fur and can work their way into the nose, ears, eyes, paws and skin, and they do not come out on their own.

Sudden sneezing, head shaking, pawing at the face, a swollen or closed eye or limping after a walk can all be signs. Call us if you suspect a foxtail.

Questions

Urgent care FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

Do I need an appointment for urgent care?

Please call (310) 310-3704 first during business hours. Telling us what is happening lets us prepare and see your pet as quickly as possible.

What should I do if my pet has an emergency at night or on the weekend?

Go to Veterinary Emergency Group – Santa Monica at 2700 Wilshire Boulevard, Santa Monica, CA 90403. They are open 24 hours a day and their phone number is (424) 538-4535.

Can my pet have surgery the same day if it is needed?

Often, yes. If a procedure is needed, we can most likely perform it in our own hospital, and we offer same-day surgery when it is medically appropriate.

My dog swallowed something. What should I do?

Call us right away and keep the packaging of whatever was swallowed. Do not try to make your dog vomit unless a veterinarian tells you to.

What are the signs that my pet needs urgent care?

Breathing difficulty, pale or yellow gums, sudden vomiting or diarrhea, straining to urinate, sudden lameness, a swollen eye, visible pain, foxtails or swallowing an object are all reasons to call us the same day.
