Your pet's surgery, from consultation to recovery

  1. 1

    Consultation

    A full examination and, when needed, x-rays to reach a diagnosis and explain your options.
  2. 2

    Plan

    Pre-anesthetic bloodwork, a clear quote and instructions to prepare your pet, including fasting.
  3. 3

  4. 4

    Recovery

    Most patients go home the same day or the next, with rechecks and physical therapy if needed.

Safety and comfort

Anesthesia and pain control come first

We use ventilators and advanced anesthesia protocols, which matter most for patients with breathing challenges such as bulldogs and pugs. Surgical devices that minimize bleeding shorten anesthesia time and reduce pain after surgery.

  • Epidurals for hind-limb procedures, blocking pain for 24 hours
  • Nocita®, a local anesthetic that keeps the surgical area comfortable for up to 72 hours
  • Pre-anesthetic bloodwork and a physical exam before every procedure
  • Overnight monitoring available on select days

Questions

Veterinary surgery FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

What types of surgery do you perform?

Orthopedic surgery such as TPLO, fracture repair and FHNO; soft tissue surgery of the head and neck, abdomen and chest; laparoscopic procedures such as spays and gastropexy; and airway surgery for brachycephalic breeds.

Who performs the surgery?

Surgery is led by Dr. Antonio Pedraza, DVM, MVM, cert SAS, MRCVS, who has more than 20 years of surgical experience and postgraduate surgical training from the United Kingdom and Spain.

How is pain managed after surgery?

Pain management is a priority: epidurals block pain for 24 hours after hind-limb procedures, and Nocita®, a long-acting local anesthetic, keeps the surgical area comfortable for up to 72 hours.

Will my pet go home the same day?

Many patients go home the same day or the day after surgery. Overnight monitoring is available on select days for pets that need extended care.

Do you offer financing or accept pet insurance?

We offer financing options to make surgical care more accessible, and we work with all pet insurance carriers, helping with documentation and claims whenever possible.

Do you accept surgical referrals from other veterinarians?

Yes. We offer free consultations for potential surgical referrals and send detailed reports to the referring veterinarian at every step.
