Meet our veterinarians

Three veterinarians, one way of caring

Founder and veterinarian

Dr. Antonio Pedraza Reales

DVM, MVM, cert SAS, MRCVS

Dr. Pedraza has had a lifelong passion for animals, which led him to a career in veterinary medicine. After obtaining his veterinary license, he moved to the United Kingdom for postgraduate training with ESPVS (European School of Postgraduate Veterinary Studies) and BSAVA (British Small Animal Veterinary Association), focused primarily on orthopedics, spinal and complex soft tissue surgery.

He received extensive training in endoscopy and laparoscopy, and earned a Master's degree in Small Animal Surgery & Traumatology in Madrid. He then moved to Los Angeles with his wife, a California native, and has since provided his services to hospitals in and around Los Angeles before founding Sevilla Veterinary Hospital, named after his hometown of Sevilla, Spain.

Dr. Pedraza believes the key to good veterinary medicine is combining clinical skill with good communication. He is attuned to the needs of his patients and their families, encourages open conversation and a supportive atmosphere, and firmly believes this leads to better long-term outcomes for every patient.

Veterinarian

Dr. Gabriella Robbins

DVM

Dr. Robbins is a dedicated, compassionate veterinarian serving the Santa Monica community she has always called home. Born and raised in Los Angeles, she brings a deep connection to the people and pets of the area.

She earned her Doctor of Veterinary Medicine at Ross University School of Veterinary Medicine in 2018 and completed her clinical training at Oregon State University, where she honed her skills in diagnostics, preventive care, internal medicine and soft tissue surgery.

Dr. Robbins believes every pet deserves to be treated like family, and every client deserves honesty, empathy and clear communication. She builds personalized treatment plans in partnership with owners, stays engaged in continuing education and gives back through educational outreach and local events. Outside the clinic you will find her enjoying the Santa Monica sunshine with her own pets or exploring the local food scene.

Veterinarian

Dr. Holly Jordan

DVM

Dr. Jordan grew up in rural Ohio, where her love for animals started early while helping care for stray animals near her family's home. She earned her veterinary degree from Western University of Health Sciences in Southern California and has spent the past several years practicing small animal medicine in the Los Angeles area.

She enjoys every aspect of general practice, with particular interests in internal medicine, preventive care and long-term relationships with pets and their owners. She is known for her thorough approach and compassionate care, and works with several rescues and fosters regularly.

Outside of work, Dr. Jordan enjoys hiking, mountain biking, travel and time with family and friends. She also has a passion for innovation in the pet industry and co-developed Barkly, a dog leash designed with pet and owner safety in mind.

Who are the veterinarians at Sevilla Veterinary Hospital?
