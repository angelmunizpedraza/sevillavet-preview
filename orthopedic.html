Cruciate repair: TPLO, TTA, CBLO

Torn cruciate ligament

The cruciate ligaments are two bands of fibrous tissue inside each knee that join the femur and the tibia. Unlike the ACL in people, the cruciate ligament in dogs is load-bearing, because a dog's leg stays bent while standing. When it tears, the knee becomes unstable and painful.

Injuries can happen suddenly, from jumping or turning too quickly, or develop slowly as the ligament degenerates with age. The repair technique is chosen from your dog's history, weight, age and breed.

  • Hind leg lameness and limping
  • Stiffness after rest following exercise
  • Swelling around the knee
  • Difficulty rising and jumping

Other conditions

Patellar luxation, fractures and hip problems

  • Medial patellar luxation (MPL)

    The kneecap normally glides in a groove at the end of the thigh bone. When it slips out, dogs often “skip”, carrying the hind leg for a few steps until it pops back in. Diagnosis is made with an orthopedic assessment and x-rays.

  • Fracture repair

    Fractures are common in dogs and cats, often after trauma such as being hit by a car. Life-threatening injuries are treated first; x-rays then guide the repair with plates and screws, pins inside the bone or external frames. With the right treatment most fractures heal well.

  • FHNO (hip)

    Femoral head and neck osteotomy removes the “ball” of the hip joint so the bones no longer rub. It relieves pain from hip fractures, severe arthritis or Legg-Perthes disease, and most pets return to normal activity. Smaller pets usually do especially well, as the false joint carries less weight.

Questions

Orthopedic surgery FAQ

Still have questions? Call (310) 310-3704.

What orthopedic surgeries do you perform?

Cruciate ligament repair (TPLO, TTA and CBLO), medial patellar luxation, fracture repair, femoral head and neck osteotomy (FHNO), spinal surgery, arthrodesis, hip and elbow dysplasia, dislocations and limb deformities.

How soon will my dog use the leg after surgery?

Typically, Dr. Pedraza's patients begin using the operated limb within a few days.

How is pain controlled after orthopedic surgery?

Every hind-limb procedure includes an epidural that blocks pain for 24 hours, and every orthopedic surgery includes Nocita®, a local anesthetic that provides comfort for up to 72 hours.

Why do you use titanium implants and bone grafts?

Titanium implants are strong and rarely need to be removed, and a bone graft is included with every orthopedic procedure to accelerate healing.

What is FHNO surgery?

A femoral head and neck osteotomy removes the ball of the hip joint so the bones no longer rub. Muscles and scar tissue form a false joint, relieving pain from fractures, severe arthritis or Legg-Perthes disease.

Do you perform orthopedic surgery on cats?

Yes. Fractures are common in both dogs and cats, and procedures such as fracture repair and FHNO are performed in cats too.

How do I book an orthopedic assessment?

Call (310) 310-3704. Referring veterinarians can also send cases directly, with free consultations for potential surgical referrals.
